# Privacy Policy for Flixy

**Last updated: 20 September 2026**

## Summary

Flixy does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on our servers. We
operate no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. Everything you do in
Flixy stays on your device, except for optional syncing through your own
private iCloud account and the requests the app makes directly to the media
providers and metadata services described below.

## 1. Who we are

Flixy is developed and operated by **Mounir Bounab** ("we", "us"), an
independent developer. For any privacy question you can reach us at
**clippingit34@gmail.com**.

## 2. What Flixy is

Flixy is a media player and playlist parser for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. It
ships completely empty and provides no content of its own. You supply your own
sources — an M3U/M3U8 playlist, an Xtream Codes account, or your own Plex
server. Flixy simply plays what you point it at.

## 3. Data we collect

**None.** Specifically, Flixy has:

- no user accounts, sign-up or login to Flixy itself;
- no analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs;
- no advertising, ad identifiers (IDFA) or tracking of any kind;
- no collection of your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or
  device identifiers.

We never see your playlists, your credentials, what you watch, or when you
watch it, because none of that information is ever sent to us.

## 4. Data stored on your device

Flixy stores the following locally on your device so that the app works:

- Playlists you add, including any server address, username, password or Plex
  access token you enter.
- Favourites, tags, playback progress, playback queue, watch history, hidden
  items and "continue watching" state.
- App settings, including parental-control settings and your PIN.
- Cached data: the downloaded playlist file, the TV guide (EPG) database,
  artwork and metadata caches.
- Any media you explicitly download for offline viewing, and any recordings you
  make.

This data is held in the app's own private container, protected by iOS/tvOS app
sandboxing and device encryption. Downloaded media files are additionally
written with iOS file protection enabled. Deleting the app removes all of it.
You can also clear caches at any time from **Settings → Storage**.

Credentials are stored in the app's private container rather than the iOS
Keychain. They are protected by the system sandbox and device encryption, and
are never transmitted anywhere other than to the server you entered them for.

## 5. Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on **Settings → Sync → iCloud sync**, Flixy writes your playlists,
favourites, playback progress, hidden items and parental settings to your own
private iCloud key-value store so they are available on your other devices.

- This uses **your** Apple Account. The data goes to Apple, not to us.
- We have no access to it and cannot read it.
- Note that playlists include any credentials you entered, so those are
  included in the sync.
- Turning the setting off stops future syncing. Removing the app's iCloud data
  is done through your Apple Account settings.

Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

## 6. Third parties Flixy connects to

Flixy makes network requests only to the following, and only as needed:

- **Your own media sources.** The M3U URL, Xtream Codes server or Plex server
  you configured. These receive your requests, your credentials (where you
  supplied them), your IP address and standard connection metadata. These
  services are operated by third parties or by you, not by us, and their
  handling of your data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
  **Choose your providers carefully — we have no relationship with any of
  them.**
- **The Movie Database (TMDB)** — `api.themoviedb.org` and `image.tmdb.org`.
  Used only to fetch artwork, descriptions, cast and trailer links that match
  the titles in your own playlist. Only the title text being matched is sent.
  No account or personal data is involved. This product uses the TMDB API but
  is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
- **TheIntroDB** — `api.theintrodb.org`. Optional. Used at playback to fetch
  intro/recap/credits timestamps for a matched title so the "Skip Intro" and
  "Next Episode" buttons can appear. Only the matched title identifier is sent,
  and results are cached for the session only.
- **YouTube** — only if you tap a trailer. The trailer opens in YouTube or your
  browser, at which point YouTube's and Google's privacy policies apply.
- **Apple** — for iCloud sync (if enabled), local notifications, AirPlay and
  standard OS services.

Flixy sends nothing to any endpoint operated by us, because we operate none.

## 7. Notifications

If you set a programme reminder, Flixy asks for notification permission and
schedules a **local** notification on your device. No push server is involved
and no notification data leaves your device.

## 8. Children

Flixy is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any data from
anyone, including children. Because you supply your own content sources, the
material shown in the app is outside our control. Flixy includes parental
controls (**Settings → Parental control**): a PIN, automatic locking of
adult-flagged categories, the option to hide locked categories entirely, and a
"Show adult content" switch. We recommend parents configure these before
handing the device to a child.

## 9. Content

Flixy does not host, provide, curate, index, resell or link to any streaming
content. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to access any
source you add to the app.

## 10. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. To
remove the data held on your device, delete the app. To remove synced data, use
your Apple Account settings. Caches can be cleared from **Settings → Storage**,
and watch history from **Settings → Sync**.

## 11. Your rights

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws you have rights to
access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its
processing. Because we do not collect or process any personal data, we hold
nothing to disclose or erase. If you believe otherwise, contact us at
**clippingit34@gmail.com** and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell
or share personal information.
