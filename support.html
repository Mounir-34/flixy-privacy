Before you write in

Flixy is a player, not a content service. It ships completely empty and plays whatever source you point it at — an M3U/M3U8 playlist, an Xtream Codes account, or your own Plex server. Most problems turn out to be with the source rather than with the app, so it is worth checking the FAQ below first.

We cannot help you find, buy or fix an IPTV subscription. We do not sell, host, curate or recommend any content, and we have no relationship with any provider. Questions of that kind will not get a useful answer.

Getting started

  1. Open Flixy and go to Settings → Playlists → Add playlist.
  2. Choose your source type:
    • M3U / M3U8 — paste the full playlist URL, or import a local file.
    • Xtream Codes — enter the server address, username and password.
    • Plex — sign in to your own Plex server and pick the libraries you want.
  3. Wait for the first load to finish. A large playlist can take a minute or two the first time while channels, artwork and the TV guide are parsed.
  4. Optionally turn on Settings → Sync → iCloud sync to carry your playlists, favourites and progress across your devices.

Frequently asked questions

My playlist won’t load

  • Open the URL in Safari on the same device. If Safari cannot reach it either, the problem is the source or your network, not Flixy.
  • Check the address is complete and uses http:// or https://, with no stray spaces from copy-and-paste.
  • Re-check the username and password for Xtream accounts — they are case-sensitive.
  • Some providers limit how many devices or connections can be active at once. Close the playlist on your other devices and try again.
  • If you are on a VPN or a restrictive network, try switching it off or moving to another network.
  • Still stuck? Settings → Playlists → remove the playlist and add it again.

A channel buffers, stutters or won’t play

  • Try another channel. If only one channel is affected, that stream is down at the provider’s end.
  • Test your connection speed on the same device. Live streams need a steady connection, not just a fast one — Wi-Fi congestion is a common cause.
  • On Apple TV, a wired Ethernet connection is noticeably more reliable than Wi-Fi for live TV.
  • Some streams use formats Apple’s player does not support. If a channel never plays anywhere while others are fine, it is likely the stream’s codec or container.

The TV guide (EPG) is empty or wrong

  • The guide comes from your provider, not from us. If they supply no EPG data, or supply it for different channel IDs, the guide will be empty or mismatched.
  • Give the first import time to finish — large EPG files take a while.
  • Try Settings → Storage → clear the guide cache, then reload the playlist.

Artwork, descriptions or cast are missing

Artwork and metadata are matched against The Movie Database (TMDB) using the title text in your playlist. If a title is spelled unusually, abbreviated or padded with provider prefixes, there may be nothing to match. Cleaning up the names on the provider’s side generally fixes it.

“Skip Intro” or “Next Episode” never appears

These rely on timestamps from TheIntroDB for a matched title. If the episode is not in that database, or the title could not be matched, the buttons simply will not show. Nothing is broken.

My Plex server doesn’t show up

  • Confirm the server is online and reachable from the Plex app on the same device.
  • Remote access must be enabled on the Plex server for connections from outside your home network.
  • Sign out and back in under Settings → Playlists to refresh the access token.

I forgot my parental-control PIN

The PIN is stored only on your device and we have no way to recover or reset it remotely. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it, along with the rest of the local data. If iCloud sync is on, turn it off before reinstalling, otherwise the old settings can sync back.

iCloud sync isn’t working

  • Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled.
  • Turn Settings → Sync → iCloud sync on for each device you want included.
  • Sync is not instant. Give it a minute, and make sure the app has been opened on the second device.

How do I free up space?

Go to Settings → Storage to see what Flixy is using and clear the playlist, guide, artwork and metadata caches. Downloads and recordings are removed from their own sections. Deleting the app removes everything.

Do you offer refunds?

All purchases go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can you add a feature?

Requests are welcome — email them in. We read every one, though we can’t promise a timeline, and anything that would involve providing or indexing content is out of scope.

Reporting a bug

To get to the bottom of a problem quickly, please include:

  • your device and OS version (for example “iPhone 15, iOS 18.2” or “Apple TV 4K, tvOS 18.2”);
  • the Flixy version number, shown at the bottom of Settings;
  • the source type you are using (M3U, Xtream Codes or Plex);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot or screen recording if the problem is visual.

Please do not send us your playlist URL, username, password or Plex token. We never need them, and we would rather not receive them.
